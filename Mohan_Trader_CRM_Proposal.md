## 1. Executive Summary

Mohan Trader requires a robust, centralized platform to manage the entire lifecycle of its vehicle trading business. Standard market solutions fail to provide the combined power of human resources, advanced financial tracking, and intelligent customer engagement tailored exclusively to the automotive sector. 

We have developed a **bespoke, state-of-the-art CRM** built from the ground up for Mohan Trader. By combining Artificial Intelligence with omnichannel lead capture (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram), this platform ensures that **no lead is ever lost, no staff action goes untracked, and financial health is always transparent.**

This proposal outlines the platform's core functional modules, the deployment investment, and ongoing technical maintenance.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Feature Breakdown

The custom CRM replaces the functional need for three separate expensive SaaS products by consolidating Operations, Finance, and Human Resources.

### 2.1 Omnichannel Lead Engine & AI WhatsApp Assistant
*Our intelligent system replaces manual chat monitoring by placing an AI agent on the frontline of your digital communications.*

* **Universal Inbox:** Automatically captures leads from WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok directly into the CRM.
* **AI Conversational Bot:** An AI-powered virtual assistant capable of greeting customers 24/7, answering questions about specific vehicles (price, mileage, features), and qualifying lead intent in both English and Sinhala structures.
* **Pipeline Management:** Sales staff view a dynamic pipeline to funnel leads from `New` -> `Contacted` -> `Test Drive` -> `Sale Completed`.

> ![Screenshot: Omnichannel Chat Interface with AI Bot Reply](/absolute/path/to/placeholder/ss1_chat.png)

### 2.2 Vehicle Inventory & Sales Optimization
*Precision stock tracking ensures total oversight over vehicle margins and availability.*

* **Deep Cost Tracking:** Log the base purchase price, transport costs, repair/refurbishment costs, and registration fees against every vehicle.
* **Live Stock Sync:** Deducts inventory automatically the moment a Lead's status moves to "Sale Completed".
* **Automated Commission calculation:** Assign sales agents to closed deals, instantly calculating their sales commissions based on company configurations.

> ![Screenshot: Vehicle Inventory Dashboard with Costs Breakdown](/absolute/path/to/placeholder/ss2_inventory.png)

### 2.3 Finance, Ledgers & Profit Analysis
*Eliminate spreadsheets. An automated, real-time Profit & Loss engine designed for the high-ticket automotive space.*

* **Real-time ROI & P&L Engine:** Automatically calculate Net Business Profit by deducting actual inventory costs and mapped operational expenses from gross vehicle sales.
* **Enterprise PDF Audits:** Generate one-click, beautifully branded PDF Financial Audit Reports (with company logo and slogan) directly from the dashboard for accountants or investors.
* **Dual-Ledger Accounting:** Separate Cash Flow registers dynamically track balances across virtual "Bank" and "Petty Cash" accounts.

> ![Screenshot: Professional P&L Financial Report Export Screen](/absolute/path/to/placeholder/ss3_finance.png)

### 2.4 Human Resources & Staff Accountability
*Designed to keep dealership staff accountable, motivated, and structurally organized.*

* **GPS-Verified Attendance:** Staff can clock in and out using their mobile devices, automatically storing precise Geographic Coordinates to prevent time-theft.
* **Internal Notice Board:** Management can publish rich-text announcements directly to staff dashboards.
* **Leave Management:** Employees can request off-days digitally for management approval.

> ![Screenshot: Geo-Tagged HR Attendance & Check-in Dashboard](/absolute/path/to/placeholder/ss4_hr.png)

## 4. Ongoing Maintenance & Operations (Recurring)

To keep cloud servers running fast, secure databases, and guarantee uptime for the AI and WhatsApp services, a monthly maintenance agreement is required.

### Option A: Standard Maintenance (Rs. 30,000 / month)
*Best for ensuring stable, day-to-day operations.*
* **Cloud Infrastructure & Database Hosting Fees** (Frontend, Backend, and DB compute).
* **Automated Daily Backups** of all leads, inventory, and financial ledgers.
* **Continuous Uptime Monitoring** & bug fixes for any core system faults.
* Technical administration of Meta API and OpenAI API connections.

### Option B: Premium "Growth" Maintenance (Rs. 75,000 / month)
*Best if you anticipate aggressive growth and want continuous software improvements.*
* Everything in the *Standard Maintenance* package, PLUS:
* **Priority Escalation Support:** Direct technical line with a 2-hour response guarantee.
* **10 Hours of Custom Development per Month:** Use this to request new feature modules across the platform.
